## Review of Last Month

Last month's Project of the Month was [Project Zero Inventory](https://shep4.com/blog/2026/07/project-zero-inventory-w1/). The idea was to sell items on my website store without having to keep any inventory on hand and instead relying on on-demand 3D printing. The reason this is interesting is because it significantly lowers the barrier to entry for new product ideas. Instead of relying on bulk manufacturing with expensive minimum orders before demand for the product has been demonstrated, I can instead test the market with lower quality 3D printed versions. If people buy the product and demonstrate demand then I can iterate and put out an order for a higher quality version knowing that people actually will want it. And if nobody buys the product I can move on without sinking any money into it.

Anyway, that was the project idea, but I ran into a major limitation: I don't actually own a 3D printer. I have a friend with a 3D printer, and he said I could use it, but then timing didn't work out and I was unable to print anything last month. For this reason, I'm going to extend Project Zero Inventory into August.

This was a learning opportunity for me because I realized that if I want to build projects in public each month, I need to pick projects that don't have constraints that are out of my control, or better yet, I can present projects I have already finished.

## Moving Forward
