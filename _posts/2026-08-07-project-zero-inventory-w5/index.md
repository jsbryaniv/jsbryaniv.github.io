If you have been following along with my Project of the Month series, then you might have noticed I haven't posted about it for the past few weeks. First off, if you noticed that, then thanks for noticing! Second, let me explain what happened.

## The Dilemma

Last month I introduced [Project Zero Inventory](https://shep4.com/blog/2026/07/project-zero-inventory-w1/). The idea is that I wanted to experiment with on-demand manufacturing as a way to test out market demand for a product before sinking money into a costly minimum order. I have a friend with a 3D printer, and he said I could use it to experiment with the idea.

But then life got in the way!

I created a few designs for 3D printing, but due to a few unexpected circumstances, I was not able to print any of them.

So I definitely learned from that. Moving forward, if I want to pick a Project of the Month that I build in public, I need to make sure that the project doesn't depend too heavily on factors outside of my control. Or better yet, I can present finished projects each month, writing about them retrospectively.

## Project of the Month... Again.

So with all that in mind, I'm extending Project Zero Inventory into August. Part of this will be printing and selling the designs I made last month, but part of this will be expanding the zero inventory philosophy into new product ideas.

Stay tuned for next week, where I promise you I will have a real product to show!
